# CBR Praktijkexamen B — Exam Structure

> Source: CBR, Rijprocedure personenauto (B), versie juli 2026.
> URL: https://www.cbr.nl/nl/voor-rijscholen/nl/rijprocedures/rijprocedure-b-1

The CBR practical exam for the passenger-car licence (categorie B) assesses whether
the candidate can participate in traffic independently and responsibly. The exam is
driven against the Rijprocedure B, which describes the most desirable driving
behaviour and is the yardstick the examiner uses.

## Exam parts (examenonderdelen)

The exam covers the **onderdelen van verkeersdeelneming** (Rijprocedure B, §3):

1. **Wegrijden** (pulling away) — §3.1, especially pulling away from a parking
   position (a bijzondere manoeuvre).
2. **Rijden op rechte en bochtige weggedeelten** (driving straight and curved roads)
   — §3.2.
3. **Gedrag nabij en op kruispunten** (intersections) — §3.3, including turning
   left/right, priority, traffic lights.
4. **Invoegen - uitvoegen** (merging and exiting) — §3.4, mainly on motorways.
5. **Inhalen / zijdelings verplaatsen** (overtaking and lane changes) — §3.5.
6. **Gedrag nabij en op bijzondere weggedeelten** (special road sections) — §3.6,
   including roundabouts, erf, level crossings, pedestrian crossings, inrit/uitrit.
7. **Bijzondere verrichtingen** (special manoeuvres) — §3.7. The examiner picks
   about two from: parallel parking (fileparkeren), bay parking forward/reverse
   (parkeervak), turning around / three-point turn (omkeren), reversing in a curve
   (achteruit bocht), hill start (hellingproef), stopping assignment (stopopdracht).

## How the exam parts are scored

Chapters 1 (vehicle control) and 2 (safe traffic participation) have a
'paraplufunctie': they apply to every exam part in chapter 3. So the examiner takes
the chapter-1 and chapter-2 topics into account at every part.

Each exam part has **essential** assessment topics (essentiële onderwerpen van
beoordeling) and non-essential ones. See Rijprocedure B, §Toepassing, and the topic
file `assessment_criteria` for how deviations are weighed (the AEX factors: aard,
ernst, aantal malen).

## What the examiner decides

The examiner decides whether the candidate is **rijvaardig** (fit to drive). The
total picture matters: central is whether it is responsible to let the candidate
participate in traffic independently. If the examiner marks one or more parts as
onvoldoende after the final weighing, the exam result is onvoldoende (failed).

See also: `bijzondere_verrichtingen` (the special manoeuvres) and
`assessment_criteria` (how errors are scored).
