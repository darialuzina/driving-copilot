# CBR Bijzondere Verrichtingen (Special Manoeuvres)

> Source: CBR, Rijprocedure personenauto (B), versie juli 2026, §3.7.
> URL: https://www.cbr.nl/nl/voor-rijscholen/nl/rijprocedures/rijprocedure-b-1

Bijzondere verrichtingen are the manoeuvres the examiner assigns during the practical
exam; the examiner chooses about two. They test **vehicle control and mastery in a
limited space**.

## The manoeuvres (Rijprocedure B, §3.7)

- **Fileparkeren** — parallel parking (along the kerb, between two parked cars).
- **Parkeervak** — bay parking, forward or reverse (into a parking bay).
- **Omkeren** — turning the car around, e.g. a three-point turn (driepuntsdraai).
- **Achteruit bocht** — reversing in a curve / along a curved road.
- **Hellingproef** — hill start (pulling away on an uphill slope without rolling
  back; also stopping and pulling away on a hill).
- **Stopopdracht** — stopping assignment: stopping the car at a prescribed place
  along the road as the examiner instructs.

## Assessment rules for bijzondere verrichtingen

- **Vehicle control in a limited space** is central. The examiner judges the
  candidate's mastery of the car within a confined area.
- An **automatically inparkeersysteem** (auto-park assist) may NOT be used during the
  bijzondere verrichtingen — the examiner must see the candidate operate and master
  the vehicle themselves. (Rijprocedure B, §Toepassing Inleiding.)
- 'Hands-off, eyes-on' systems may not be used at all during the exam.
- **Beheersing van het voertuig** is essential. Crucially, where at other exam parts
  insufficient vehicle control must be **structural** (across multiple parts, or
  frequently within one part) to count as verwijtbaar (blameworthy), at the bijzondere
  verrichtingen insufficient control can already lead to an onvoldoende for that part
  on its own. (Rijprocedure B, §Toepassing Hoofdstuk 1.)
- A candidate may, under certain conditions, redo a manoeuvre once; repeated failure
  weighs more heavily.

## Other essential topics at this part

As at every chapter-3 part, the chapter-1 and chapter-2 topics (belangen andere
weggebruikers, aangepast/besluitvaardig gedrag) apply. The essential topics specific
to bijzondere verrichtingen centre on: belangen andere weggebruikers, kijkgedrag,
plaats op de weg/plaats van handeling, and speed adapted to the manoeuvre.
